# Personal ApplyPilot data

`profile.json` is the canonical factual candidate profile used by ApplyPilot.
It contains the complete inventory of education, work history, historical work,
qualifications, certifications, skills, projects, privacy flags, and tailoring
metadata.

## Resume files

`resumes/resume_technical.txt` and `resumes/resume_communication.txt` are
legacy/reference exports from source PDFs. They are not authoritative and are
kept for evidence/reference while tailoring moves toward the canonical profile.

## Certifications

`certifications/` contains source certificate documents. The certification
record in `profile.json` is the structured source of truth for eligibility and
resume selection.
